Our Work

Programmes designed around real professional gaps.

WADFIN's programme model connects capability, research, standards, legal readiness, mentorship and public-private cooperation.

WADFIN Academy

Short courses, workshops, practical labs and train-the-trainer programmes covering digital-evidence handling, computer and mobile forensics, cloud evidence, OSINT, incident response, reporting, tool validation and courtroom readiness.

Practitioner Exchange

Trusted professional forums, regional meet-ups, case-study sessions and specialist working groups for lawful, non-sensitive knowledge exchange. The objective is to make it easier for practitioners to find peers with relevant expertise without turning WADFIN into a case-sharing platform.

Open Evidence Lab

A public-facing repository for open-source tools, synthetic test datasets, validation resources, forensic artefacts, checklists and practical technical guides. Confidential case material will not be hosted.

Standards & Practice Initiative

Practical templates, evidence-handling guidance and competency pathways aligned with recognised forensic principles and applicable law. WADFIN's role is to support dialogue and professional practice rather than issue unsupported claims of accreditation.

Legal & Policy Observatory

Comparative research into cybercrime law, evidence, privacy, data protection, interception, search and seizure, mutual legal assistance and foreign-held digital evidence across West Africa.

African Digital Forensics Research Forum

A practitioner–university platform for research collaboration on emerging forensic challenges relevant to African environments, digital services and justice systems.

Mentorship & Inclusion

Structured mentorship for early-career investigators, students and underrepresented professionals, with deliberate pathways for women entering digital forensics and technical investigation.

Industry & Justice Partnership

Structured engagement with banks, fintech companies, telecoms, technology platforms, prosecutors, regulators, universities and forensic technology providers to strengthen lawful cooperation and capacity.

Connecting investigators. Strengthening evidence. Advancing justice.

WADFIN is in its founding phase and is being developed as an independent, professional West African collaboration network.
