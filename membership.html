Membership

Join a professional community built around trust, competence and contribution.

WADFIN membership is being designed for practitioners, researchers, educators, students and allied professionals committed to ethical digital-forensics practice.

Proposed membership pathways

Professional Member

For qualified or suitably experienced digital forensic, cybercrime and investigative practitioners.

Associate Member

For justice, legal, cybersecurity, regulatory and allied professionals who support the digital-evidence ecosystem.

Student / Early-Career

For supervised learners and emerging practitioners seeking mentorship, learning and professional connection.

Academic / Research

For educators, researchers and university partners contributing to applied forensic knowledge.

Institutional Member

For agencies, universities, professional bodies and organisations seeking structured engagement.

Founding Contributor

For experienced practitioners and institutions helping shape WADFIN's professional and technical agenda during establishment.

Membership principles

  • Professional conduct and evidence integrity
  • Lawful authority and respect for privacy
  • No unauthorised sharing of case material or operational intelligence
  • Respectful cross-border professional collaboration
  • Transparency around conflicts of interest
  • Commitment to learning, validation and responsible knowledge exchange
