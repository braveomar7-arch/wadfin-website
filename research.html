Planned resources

  • Digital evidence acquisition and preservation checklists
  • Chain-of-custody and examination documentation templates
  • Synthetic forensic datasets for safe training and validation
  • Tool-validation notes and reproducibility guidance
  • West African digital-evidence legal and policy briefs
  • African practitioner research publications and conference proceedings
Professional boundary: WADFIN resources will not include confidential case material, unlawfully obtained data, operational intelligence or content that would facilitate wrongdoing.
