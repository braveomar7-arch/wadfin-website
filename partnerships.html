Partnerships

Build regional capability with us.

WADFIN is seeking partners who want to strengthen professional capability, research, evidence quality, cooperation and institutional resilience.

Why partner with WADFIN?

The network is designed as a collaboration platform: connecting people and institutions that already hold expertise, creating scalable programmes and ensuring African professionals are participants and co-creators in regional and international digital-forensics work.

Government & law enforcement

Professional development, regional specialist access, training pathways, standards dialogue and stronger evidence readiness.

Prosecutors & justice institutions

Digital-evidence literacy, stronger reporting, understanding of technical limitations and courtroom readiness.

Universities

Research partnerships, practitioner access, student pathways, curriculum input and applied research opportunities.

Financial institutions & fintech

Structured collaboration around fraud evidence, incident response and lawful information sharing.

Telecoms & technology providers

Responsible investigator engagement, technical research and capacity-building partnerships.

Funders & development partners

A regional platform with measurable outcomes in skills, evidence practice, representation, cooperation and sustainability.

Founding partnership priorities

  • Governance and formal establishment
  • WADFIN Academy pilot training and mentorship cohorts
  • Open Evidence Lab and validation resources
  • Legal & Digital Evidence Observatory research
  • University and practitioner research partnerships
  • Founding practitioner roundtables and regional conference development
  • Digital platform and secure professional collaboration infrastructure
