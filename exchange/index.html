Practitioner Exchange

A trusted space for practitioners to learn from practitioners.

Technical roundtables, communities of practice and regional peer exchange focused on practical digital evidence challenges.

What the Exchange does

The Practitioner Exchange provides recurring professional spaces for technical discussion and peer learning. Sessions are designed around practice problems rather than organisational status.

Technical Roundtables

Focused discussions on artefacts, tools, validation, workflows and emerging technology.

Communities of Practice

Topic groups for mobile, computer, cloud, OSINT, quality, first response and related specialties.

Case Learning

Sanitised or simulated scenarios used to examine decisions, documentation and lessons without exposing protected material.

Annual activity cycle

  • Quarterly regional technical roundtable
  • Monthly or bi-monthly specialist community sessions as capacity permits
  • Country and institution spotlight sessions
  • Tool demonstrations and validation discussions
  • Annual practitioner forum bringing multiple disciplines together

Participation principles

No live case material, protected intelligence or personal data should be shared unless a lawful and specifically approved mechanism exists. Discussion should focus on methods, learning and professional development.

Host or contribute a session

Practitioners and institutions can propose technical topics, speakers or collaborative sessions.

Propose a session

Connecting investigators. Strengthening evidence. Advancing justice.

WADFIN is an emerging practitioner-led professional network building stronger connections, capability and knowledge across the digital evidence community in West Africa.
