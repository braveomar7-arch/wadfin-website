Standards & Practice

Practical guidance for reliable digital evidence.

Resources and professional dialogue focused on integrity, validation, documentation, quality and clear communication.

Practice framework

WADFIN's Standards & Practice Initiative helps practitioners translate established digital evidence principles into clear, usable workflows. It does not create a substitute legal standard. Institutions remain responsible for applicable law, policy and accreditation requirements.

Identify & preserve

Recognition of digital evidence, scene considerations, preservation, seizure, packaging and continuity.

Acquire & process

Documented acquisition choices, verification, working copies, tool configuration and controlled processing.

Analyse & interpret

Method selection, corroboration, validation, uncertainty, limitations and peer challenge.

Report & present

Clear findings, reproducible records, exhibits, technical explanation and appropriate statement of limitations.

Quality & validate

Method validation, tool testing, competency, proficiency, corrective action and continual improvement.

Review & learn

Case review, lessons learned, method updates and knowledge transfer into future practice.

Planned resource series

  • Digital Evidence First Responder checklist
  • Examination planning template
  • Chain of custody and exhibit documentation examples
  • Tool validation and verification worksheet
  • Peer review checklist
  • Technical reporting guide
  • Laboratory readiness and quality self-assessment

Principles

Resources should be jurisdiction-aware, tool-neutral where possible, proportionate to risk, transparent about limitations and adaptable to different institutional environments.
