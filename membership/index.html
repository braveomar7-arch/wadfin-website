Membership

Join a professional community built around trust, competence and contribution.

Membership connects practitioners, justice professionals, researchers, educators, students and institutions around responsible digital evidence practice.

Membership framework

WADFIN membership is designed around professional contribution, ethical conduct and meaningful participation. Membership pathways recognise that the digital evidence ecosystem includes practitioners at different career stages as well as researchers, educators and institutions.

Professional Member

For suitably qualified or experienced digital forensic, cybercrime and investigative practitioners.

Focus:

Technical exchange, CPD, working groups, mentoring and professional contribution.

Associate Member

For legal, justice, cybersecurity, regulatory and allied professionals working with digital evidence.

Focus:

Cross-disciplinary learning, events, policy dialogue and knowledge exchange.

Student & Early-Career

For learners and emerging practitioners building competence and professional networks.

Focus:

Mentoring, learning pathways, career development and supervised contribution.

Academic & Research

For educators, researchers and research groups contributing to digital evidence knowledge.

Focus:

Research collaboration, teaching exchange, publication and student engagement.

Institutional Member

For agencies, universities, laboratories, professional bodies and aligned organisations.

Focus:

Institutional programmes, staff development, research and structured collaboration.

Founding Contributor

For experienced individuals and institutions helping shape WADFIN during its establishment and growth.

Focus:

Governance input, technical leadership and programme development.

Member experience

Learn

Access selected Academy activities, webinars, technical briefings and continuing professional development opportunities.

Connect

Participate in practitioner exchanges, communities of practice, mentoring and regional professional events.

Contribute

Join working groups, review resources, support research, teach, mentor or help develop responsible open tools.

Member commitments

  • Act lawfully and protect confidential or sensitive information
  • Respect evidence integrity and professional boundaries
  • Represent qualifications and experience accurately
  • Declare relevant conflicts of interest
  • Engage respectfully across institutions, countries and disciplines
  • Do not use WADFIN membership to imply authority, certification or endorsement that has not been granted
Membership status: Detailed eligibility rules, subscriptions, voting rights and membership terms are being finalised through WADFIN's governance process. The website does not represent unconfirmed membership benefits as guaranteed entitlements.
