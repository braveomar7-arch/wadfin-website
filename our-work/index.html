Our Work

From individual expertise to stronger professional systems.

WADFIN organises its work around capability, professional practice, knowledge, research, mentorship and institutional cooperation. Each area is designed to strengthen how digital evidence is handled, examined, explained and used in justice processes.

Strategic areas

Six connected areas of work

WADFIN is being developed as a professional network, not simply a training provider. Its programmes connect people, practice, knowledge and institutions.

1. Capability Development

Structured learning through WADFIN Academy, practical workshops, train-the-trainer programmes and institutional capacity support.

Explore WADFIN Academy →

2. Practitioner Exchange

Trusted forums, technical roundtables, regional meet-ups and specialist groups for lawful, non-sensitive professional exchange.

3. Standards & Practice

Practical guidance on evidence integrity, documentation, validation, quality, reporting and professional competence.

4. Research & Open Knowledge

Applied research, open-source tools, synthetic datasets, practice guides and publication pathways relevant to African environments.

5. Mentorship & Inclusion

Structured support for early-career professionals, students and underrepresented practitioners, including pathways for women in technical investigation.

6. Industry & Justice Partnership

Constructive engagement among investigators, prosecutors, universities, regulators, technology companies, telecoms, financial institutions and forensic providers.

Practitioner Exchange

The Practitioner Exchange is intended to make expertise easier to find across borders while preserving legal, ethical and operational boundaries. Activities can include technical seminars, peer learning sessions, thematic working groups and annual or regional professional forums.

Priority themes

  • Computer and mobile forensics
  • Cyber-enabled fraud and financial investigations
  • Browser, cloud and online evidence
  • Evidence handling and investigative workflow
  • Reporting, disclosure and courtroom communication

Target outcomes

  • Stronger professional connections across West Africa
  • Faster access to peer knowledge and specialist expertise
  • More consistent understanding of good practice
  • New practitioner-led research and training collaborations

Standards & Practice Initiative

This initiative will translate recognised forensic principles into practical resources that teams can adapt to their lawful operating environments. WADFIN will support improvement and dialogue without claiming regulatory authority or accreditation it does not hold.

Evidence Integrity

Guidance on acquisition, preservation, continuity, hashing, documentation and controlled handling.

Examination Quality

Templates for method validation, tool testing, peer review, reproducibility and examination records.

Professional Competence

Competency pathways, continuing professional development and role-based learning expectations.

Programme targets

During its establishment phase, WADFIN will use realistic, reviewable targets rather than presenting future activity as already achieved.

Regional reachBuild practitioner participation across multiple West African jurisdictions.
LearningPilot Academy programmes and gather structured participant and institutional feedback.
KnowledgePublish practical guides, research outputs and open resources that can be independently reviewed.
PartnershipsDevelop working relationships with justice institutions, universities, professional bodies and industry.
