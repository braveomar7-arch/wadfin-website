Knowledge Hub

Research, tools and practice knowledge for digital evidence.

A practitioner-facing hub for applied research, open-source tools, practice resources and policy awareness.

Knowledge Hub

The WADFIN Knowledge Hub brings research, practice resources, open-source tools and policy awareness into one professional space. Its purpose is to turn knowledge into resources that practitioners and institutions can use.

Open Evidence Lab

A collaborative space for testing methods, developing open tools, documenting artefacts and producing reproducible technical resources.

Research Forum

A bridge between practitioners and academia for applied research questions, student projects, technical seminars and publication.

Legal & Policy Observatory

A structured programme for tracking developments that affect digital evidence, cybercrime investigation, privacy and cross-border cooperation.

Research priorities

Mobile & cloud evidence

Acquisition challenges, app artefacts, cloud-linked data and interpretation in rapidly changing ecosystems.

Browser & internet artefacts

Web history, extensions, synchronisation, third-party traces and reconstruction of online activity.

Digital evidence quality

Validation, repeatability, tool testing, documentation, peer review and quality management in resource-diverse environments.

Open-source forensics

Transparent, accessible tools and methods that can be examined, tested and adapted responsibly.

AI & emerging technology

Forensic implications of generative AI, synthetic media, connected devices and new sources of electronic evidence.

Law, policy & practice

Practical implications of legislation, procedure, privacy, admissibility and cross-border evidence processes.

Research Reports

Open-source tools

ChronoTrace

An open-source Windows-focused Chromium artefact and timeline tool developed to support transparent forensic examination and reporting workflows.

Principle: open development does not replace validation. Users should test tools for their own environment and document limitations.

TraceFrame

An open-source initiative within WADFIN's broader goal of encouraging accessible, inspectable and practitioner-informed forensic tooling.

Technical documentation and release information will be published as project components are ready.

Responsible research principles

  • Lawful and ethical use of data
  • Clear separation between test data and live case material
  • Reproducible methods where practicable
  • Transparent limitations and conflicts of interest
  • Responsible disclosure of security-sensitive findings
  • Respect for privacy, confidentiality and institutional approvals

Propose research or contribute a resource

WADFIN welcomes applied research questions, technical contributions, peer reviewers and institutional research collaboration.
