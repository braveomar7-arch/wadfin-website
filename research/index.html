Knowledge Hub

Research, tools and practice knowledge for digital evidence.

The Knowledge Hub is WADFIN's platform for practitioner-led research, open technical resources, legal and policy analysis, and reproducible forensic learning.

Open Evidence Lab

Open-source tools, synthetic datasets, artefact references, validation notes and safe practical resources.

Research Forum

A practitioner and university platform for applied research on digital evidence challenges relevant to African contexts.

Legal & Policy Observatory

Comparative work on cybercrime law, evidence, privacy, data protection and cross-border cooperation.

Practice Guides

Planned guides will focus on tasks that practitioners repeatedly need to perform and explain. Each guide should identify its scope, assumptions, limitations, review date and responsible contributors.

Acquisition & Preservation

Field and laboratory checklists for documenting and preserving digital evidence.

Examination & Validation

Repeatable workflows, test approaches and documentation for transparent forensic analysis.

Reporting & Communication

Practical structures for findings, limitations, exhibits, technical review and non-technical explanation.

Open-Source Tools

ChronoTrace

An open-source Chromium browser forensic examination project developed to support examiner-controlled artefact analysis, timeline reconstruction and transparent reporting.

Focus: browser history, downloads, searches, bookmarks, metadata, extensions, sessions and supporting artefacts.

TraceFrame

An open-source forensic project initiated by WADFIN's founder. Public technical documentation and repository information will be linked when formally released.

Research priorities

  • Digital evidence practices and capability across West Africa
  • Mobile, browser, cloud and platform artefacts
  • Tool validation and reproducibility in resource-constrained laboratories
  • Cyber-enabled fraud and digital financial evidence
  • Cross-border access to digital evidence
  • Professional competence, training and forensic quality
  • Locally sustainable open-source forensic capability
Research integrity: WADFIN will distinguish published findings from proposals and works in progress. Confidential case material, unlawfully obtained data and operational intelligence will not be published.
