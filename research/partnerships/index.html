Partnerships

Build regional capability with us.

WADFIN develops practical partnerships across justice, academia, technology, professional bodies and development organisations.

Partnership model

WADFIN works with organisations that can contribute expertise, access, learning opportunities, research support, technology, venues, funding or professional pathways. Partnerships are designed around clear objectives, defined responsibilities and appropriate safeguards.

Government & justice

Police, prosecutors, judicial institutions, regulators and public agencies seeking capability development and professional exchange.

Universities & research

Institutions interested in applied research, curriculum development, student pathways and practitioner-academic collaboration.

Technology & industry

Responsible technology providers, laboratories and professional services organisations able to support tools, expertise and learning.

Professional bodies

Associations and standards communities interested in professional development, quality and cross-regional exchange.

Development partners

Organisations supporting justice, rule of law, cyber capacity, digital transformation, education and institutional resilience.

Civil society

Organisations contributing perspectives on rights, inclusion, public interest technology and responsible digital evidence practice.

Priority partnership programmes

Academy partnership

Co-design or support training, labs, instructor development, scholarships and learning infrastructure.

Research partnership

Develop studies, datasets, technical validation work, publications and practitioner-academic projects.

Equipment & tool access

Support responsible access to training equipment, software, test devices and controlled laboratory resources.

Mentorship & careers

Provide mentors, placements, career sessions and opportunities for emerging practitioners.

Regional events

Support practitioner forums, workshops, technical roundtables and the future WADFIN annual conference.

Knowledge resources

Support translation, publication, accessibility and distribution of high-quality professional resources.

How partnerships are developed

01

Explore

Identify the shared problem, intended beneficiaries and strategic fit.

02

Design

Agree scope, roles, safeguards, outputs, indicators and resource requirements.

03

Deliver

Implement with named leads, documented responsibilities and proportionate oversight.

04

Evaluate

Review outputs, outcomes, lessons, sustainability and next steps.

Partnership safeguards

  • WADFIN retains professional independence and does not provide endorsement in exchange for sponsorship
  • Commercial support should not determine technical conclusions or research findings
  • Conflicts of interest should be declared and managed
  • Case data and operational information must remain protected
  • Public claims about a partnership should reflect the agreed scope accurately
  • Programmes should include measurable outputs and a sustainability plan where appropriate
