Practitioner Exchange

The Practitioner Exchange is WADFIN's professional connection programme. It creates regular spaces for practitioners to discuss methods, emerging technologies, laboratory challenges and lessons from practice without exposing protected case information.

Planned formats

  • Quarterly technical roundtables
  • Country and institution spotlights
  • Tool and technique demonstrations
  • Case-learning sessions using sanitised or simulated scenarios
  • Special interest groups for mobile, computer, cloud, OSINT and quality practice
  • Annual regional practitioner forum

Intended outcomes

Stronger peer networks, faster knowledge exchange, more consistent practice, greater visibility of regional expertise and new opportunities for joint learning.

Standards & Practice Initiative

This programme translates recognised principles of digital evidence into accessible practice resources for different operating environments. It focuses on integrity, documentation, validation, repeatability, quality assurance and clear communication of findings.

Programme targets

Regular learning cycleMaintain a rolling calendar of technical learning and practitioner exchange activities.
Reusable resourcesPublish practical guides, templates and learning resources that institutions can adapt responsibly.
Regional participationBuild participation across multiple West African countries, sectors and career stages.
Sustainable capabilityDevelop mentors, trainers and contributors who can multiply knowledge in their own institutions.
