What to include in your enquiry

To help us respond efficiently, include your name, organisation or professional role, country, area of interest and what you would like to discuss. Do not send confidential case material, personal data from investigations or restricted operational information through general contact channels.

Professional enquiries: WADFIN welcomes contact from practitioners, government and justice institutions, universities, researchers, professional bodies, responsible technology organisations and development partners.

Connecting investigators. Strengthening evidence. Advancing justice.

WADFIN is an emerging practitioner-led professional network building stronger connections, capability and knowledge across the digital evidence community in West Africa.
