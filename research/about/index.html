About WADFIN

Connecting expertise. Building capability. Strengthening professional visibility.

WADFIN is building a trusted professional community around digital forensics, investigations and digital evidence across West Africa.

Who we are

WADFIN brings together practitioners, investigators, justice professionals, researchers, educators, students, technology specialists and institutions with a shared interest in reliable digital evidence. Our role is to connect expertise, develop practical capability and create trusted spaces for professional exchange.

Regional connection

Build relationships across countries and sectors so expertise can move more easily between practitioners, institutions and disciplines.

Professional capability

Support practical learning, mentoring, standards awareness and continuing professional development across the digital evidence lifecycle.

Knowledge and visibility

Make regional expertise, research, tools and practice lessons easier to discover, discuss and develop.

Vision

A West African digital evidence community that is connected, capable, ethical, visible and able to contribute confidently to justice, security and public trust.

Mission

To strengthen digital forensics and investigations in West Africa through professional networking, practice-led learning, research, mentorship, knowledge exchange, responsible innovation and cross-sector partnership.

Strategic pillars

1. Capability

Develop people through the WADFIN Academy, practical exercises, trainer development and institutional learning support.

2. Community

Create practitioner exchanges, mentoring relationships, technical forums and opportunities for cross-border professional connection.

3. Practice

Promote evidence integrity, repeatability, validation, documentation, disclosure awareness and responsible professional conduct.

4. Knowledge

Support research, practice guides, open tools, policy monitoring and accessible regional knowledge resources.

5. Partnership

Connect law enforcement, justice institutions, academia, civil society and responsible industry partners around shared capability needs.

6. Sustainability

Grow local expertise, trainer capacity and reusable resources so programmes can continue beyond individual projects.

Who WADFIN serves

Practitioners

Digital forensic examiners, cybercrime investigators, analysts, first responders, laboratory personnel and technical investigators.

Justice professionals

Prosecutors, legal practitioners, judicial officers, regulators and professionals who work with electronic evidence.

Learning community

Universities, researchers, educators, students and early-career professionals building the next generation of capability.

Founding story

WADFIN began from a practical observation: West Africa has skilled professionals working with digital evidence, but many remain separated by institutional, national and professional boundaries. The network was initiated to help close that connection gap and create a durable professional community around people, knowledge, collaboration and justice.

Our approach: WADFIN is practitioner-led and partnership-oriented. It does not replace national authorities, existing professional bodies or international organisations. It creates an additional professional bridge for learning, connection and locally relevant capability development.

Connecting investigators. Strengthening evidence. Advancing justice.

WADFIN is an emerging practitioner-led professional network building stronger connections, capability and knowledge across the digital evidence community in West Africa.
