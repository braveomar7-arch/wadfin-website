Mentorship & Inclusion

Developing people, not just technical skills.

Structured mentoring for students, early-career professionals and practitioners developing specialist or leadership capability.

Programme purpose

The WADFIN Mentorship Programme is designed to help emerging practitioners navigate technical learning, professional development and responsible career progression through structured relationships with experienced professionals.

Early-Career Track

For students, graduates and new practitioners seeking direction, professional habits and exposure to the field.

Practitioner Development Track

For working professionals developing a specialty, leadership skills, research capability or training experience.

Women & Inclusion Track

Focused support for participation and progression of groups that may be underrepresented in technical and investigative roles.

Six-month mentoring cycle

01

Profile

Define goals, experience, interests and development needs.

02

Match

Pair participants based on goals, specialty, availability and conflict checks.

03

Develop

Agree a simple development plan with regular conversations and practical milestones.

04

Review

Evaluate progress, lessons and possible next steps at the end of the cycle.

Expected outcomes

  • Clearer professional development goals
  • Improved awareness of ethical and technical practice
  • Stronger professional networks
  • Greater confidence in learning and career decisions
  • Pathways into research, training, specialist communities or leadership
Boundary: Mentorship is professional development, not case supervision, legal advice, recruitment assurance or certification.
